🎒 Equipment Checklist

  • ✔ High-ankle waterproof trekking shoes
  • ✔ 50L–60L rucksack with rain cover
  • ✔ Thermal inners, fleece, and down jacket
  • ✔ LED headlamp with spare batteries
  • ✔ Quick-dry towels and water bottles (2L)
  • ✔ Personal medical kit and sunscreen

🛡️ Safety Guidelines

  • Stay strictly behind the trek leader at all times.
  • Acclimatize properly and hydrate regularly to prevent AMS.
  • Do not wander away from the base camp after sunset.
  • Carry your photo ID and emergency contact details.
  • Follow "Leave No Trace" principles—pack all waste back.
